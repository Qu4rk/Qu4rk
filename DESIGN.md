## Colors

## Typography

The nameplate uses outlined Satoshi Regular. Its display size is measured in the SVG’s native coordinate space and scales with the image. Use GitHub’s body type for prose, level-two headings for sections and linked level-three headings for featured projects.

## Layout

Center the compact purple nameplate and navigation. Follow with the original introduction, selected project showcases, additional work, toolkit table, and illustrated contact footer. Let links wrap on narrow screens and scale images within the reading column.
